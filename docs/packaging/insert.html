Setup · under a minute
  1. 1Plug in SeisMonitor.
  2. 2On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network SeisMonitor-Setup.
  3. 3A setup page opens. Choose your Wi-Fi, enter the password, select a region, save.
  4. 4SeisMonitor connects and begins monitoring.

No setup page? Open 192.168.4.1 in a browser. Change network or region later from the on-screen menu.
